The production process began with writing a script in KIT Scenarist. Both the lyrics and the overall video are a parody of 'Lord Verity' (1:26-1:56) by Horror Skunx, reimagined with characters from DELTARUNE by Toby Fox.

For this adaptation, I rewrote the lyrics around ERAM, drawing on dialogue, character interactions, and narrative themes from the game. The goal was to preserve the structure and tone of the original while reframing it through ERAM's perspective.

Kris' voice was performed by me
ERAM is voiced by Jade Siproeta

Some dialogue was revised during production to better accommodate the animation's pacing and maintain consistency with the characters' personalities.

DELTARUNE and its characters are the property of Toby Fox. The 3D models and adaptations presented below are my own fan work. The accompanying sprites and illustrations are existing reference materials by the credited artists.

Kris

Kris was relatively straightforward to translate into 3D, thanks to the amount of detail available in their official artwork. I used the existing illustrations and in-game sprites as references to establish the model's proportions, silhouette, clothing, and overall appearance.

The more challenging aspects came from adapting the design for animation. I added additional bones to the rig to give the mantle more flexibility and allow for more expressive movement. I also created hidden shadow-casting geometry to preserve the intended facial shadows, helping maintain Kris's recognizable appearance under different lighting conditions.

ERAM

Unlike Kris, ERAM has relatively little visual detail in the available sprites, leaving considerably more room for interpretation. Rather than relying entirely on the limited reference material, I used the character's narrative role and visual parallels within DELTARUNE to guide the design.

ERAM frequently mirrors Ralsei, whose character design draws heavily on traditional goat-like imagery. I expanded on this resemblance by pushing ERAM toward a more Krampus-inspired appearance, emphasizing elongated horns, pronounced hooves, digitigrade legs, and a deliberately tattered robe.

This also creates a visual contrast with Ralsei. Where Ralsei has a more human-like silhouette and a neat, formal robe, ERAM's exaggerated animal features and disheveled clothing give him a more imposing and unsettling presence.

Eye Horizon

The eye horizon effect was created using a procedural shader in Blender. Rather than modelling and animating individual eye objects, I used procedural pupils and stretched image masking masking to create the appearance of eyes opening across the horizon.

The shader combines procedural shapes with a gradient-based background to establish the transition between the eyes and the surrounding sky. This approach allows me to control the appearance, spacing, and overall composition of the effect through shader parameters, rather than manually positioning and animating each element.

All lip syncing was done via shapekeys.

To achieve a more convincing pixel art aesthetic, the entire animation was rendered using an orthographic camera. Since orthographic projection removes perspective depth, I recreated the illusion of depth through scene composition, invisible shadow casters (e.g. sphere in shot 1), lattice structures, and object scaling. This allowed me to retain the flat, graphic appearance of the original game's visual style while still incorporating dimensional 3D environments with finer control. ERAM is heavily associated with CRT retro aesthetic in-game, so I added barrel distrotion, chromatic abberation, and bloom to create the impression of watching the film of a degrading retro tv.

A major focus of the animation was establishing a visual contrast between ERAM's otherworldly presence and Kris's relative powerlessness. I used exaggerated squash and stretch in ERAM's movements, while keeping Kris and the surrounding scenery comparatively grounded and physically rigid. This difference in movement helps make ERAM feel less bound by the physical world, reinforcing his mischievous and supernatural demeanor.

I also used camera angles and framing to reinforce this power dynamic. Shots looking down toward Kris emphasize their vulnerability, while low angle shots looking up at ERAM make him appear more imposing. This approach also draws from the original 'Lord Verity' music video, which uses similar framing to establish Lord Verity's dominance over the other characters. Shots that show both characters at the same present ERAM as larger when speaking despite his model and canonical size being smaller.
